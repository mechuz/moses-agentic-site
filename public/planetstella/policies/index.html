Booking policy.

Booking

Book online 24/7 at planetstella.com/book or call (917) 576-6047. You'll receive a confirmation email with a calendar invite and a private link to manage your appointment.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. You can cancel online using the "Manage booking" link in your confirmation email up to 24 hours before your appointment. Inside that window, please contact us directly at (917) 576-6047.

Pricing

Menu prices are starting prices. Your final price depends on your hair's length, thickness and the products used, and is confirmed with you before any work begins.

Reminders

You'll receive an email reminder about 24 hours before your appointment.
