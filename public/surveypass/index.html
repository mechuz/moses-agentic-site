What SurveyPass does for you

Most communities find out where they stand when the surveyor walks in. SurveyPass turns survey preparation into a daily habit, with proof.

Survey-ready every day

Problems are found and fixed by your own team daily, not discovered during the survey.

Visibility from anywhere

By 3:00 PM you know who did their rounds, what's wrong and what's being fixed, wherever you are.

Proof, not promises

Every check, finding and fix is time-stamped with who and when, for surveyors, insurers and lenders.

Fewer citations

A propped fire door, a missed freezer log, an overdue skin assessment: caught and fixed the same day.

Accountability

Each department head owns their checklist and signs it with their name every day.

Built for the phone

Opens like an app from the home screen. Big buttons, made for walking the floor. Nothing to install from an app store.

Features

Everything a department head needs on the floor, and everything leadership needs to see.

Daily department checklists

Compliance items for every department, a daily deadline with a live countdown, and late submissions flagged automatically.

Corrective-action tracking

Every deficiency gets an immediate fix, a permanent fix, an owner and a due date. Fixes are verified before they close.

Resident-safety alerts

Urgent findings stay flagged in red on every screen until they're resolved.

Live leadership dashboard

Readiness score, who's submitted, a 14-day history and the repeat findings that feed your QAPI program.

Daily results email

Leadership gets the day's results automatically every afternoon.

Surveyor binder

One click prints every checklist, signature, finding and fix for any date range.

Scheduled audits

Shift medication checks, weekly clinical reviews, monthly QAPI, fire drills and license audits, with due dates.

Your state, your checklist

Assisted living is regulated state by state. Each community's checklist is built around its own rules.

Secure logins

Every person has their own login and can change only their own department. Former staff can be shut off instantly.
