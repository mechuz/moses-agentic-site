Privacy policy.

Last updated 2026-11-15.

What we collect

When you book, we collect your name, email, phone number (optional), the service and time you choose, any notes you share, and how you found us. When you join our email list, we collect your email address.

How we use it

To confirm, remind you about and manage your appointments, to contact you about your visit, and (only if you opt in) to send studio news and openings. We never sell your information.

Analytics and advertising

We may use cookies and similar tools from services such as Google Analytics, Meta and TikTok to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our advertising. You can block these through your browser settings or ad preferences.

Service providers

Your data is stored securely with our hosting provider (Cloudflare), and booking emails are delivered through an email service provider. These providers process data only on our behalf.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. To access, correct or delete your information, email info@planetstella.com.
