The craft

Skill you can see.

More than 35 years behind the chair have taught Stella that great hair is never an accident. It's technique, patience and an eye for what suits you.

01

Precision cutting

Every cut is shaped for how your hair grows, falls and moves, from sharp bobs and pixies to men's tapers finished with scissors and clippers.

02

Color mastery

Custom formulas with Schwarzkopf Igora Royal: seamless root touch-ups, dimensional highlights, Air Touch balayage and true color corrections.

03

Healthy hair first

Keratin, hair botox, Truss bond repair and scalp therapy keep hair strong and glossy, before, during and after color.

04

Care and kindness

Every client is treated with care, from the first conversation about your hair to the final finish in the mirror.
