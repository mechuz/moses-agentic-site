FAQ

Before you come.

Do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome, but Sunday afternoons, Thursday nights and Motzei Shabbos usually sell out. Booking online holds your lanes and lets you pick them on the map.

What time do you open Motzei Shabbos?

One hour after Shabbos ends. The exact time is on this page and on the booking page every week.

Is there an age limit?

Everyone is welcome until 11 PM. After 11 on Motzei Shabbos and event nights it's 18 and over. The bar is 21 and over with ID.

Can I order food to my lane?

Yes. Scan the code on your scoring screen or use the menu page. It arrives at your lane in about 12 minutes.

Do you have bumpers and ramps?

Both, free. Ask at the desk or tick the box when you book.

Is the arcade open during events?

Yes, until 11 PM. After that it's the lanes and the stage.

Lost and found?

Front desk. We hold items for 30 days.
