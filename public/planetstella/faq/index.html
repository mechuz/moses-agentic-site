Booking & visits

How do I book an appointment?

Book online any time at planetstella.com/book: pick your service, choose an open day and time, and you'll get an instant confirmation email. You can also call (917) 576-6047.

Do you cut men's and kids' hair?

Yes. Planet Stella is a unisex studio: women, men and kids are all welcome.

How much does a haircut or color cost?

Women's haircuts start at $60+, men's at $45+ and kids' at $40+. Color starts at $80+ for a root touch-up. Final pricing depends on your hair's length and thickness. See the full menu for every service.

How do I change or cancel my appointment?

Use the "Manage booking" link in your confirmation email to cancel online up to 24 hours before your appointment, then pick a new time. Inside that window, call (917) 576-6047.

Haircuts

How often should I get a haircut?

Most clients keep their shape sharp with a cut every 6 to 10 weeks. Short styles, tapers and bangs need a trim more often.

Do you cut men's and kids' hair?

Yes. Planet Stella is a unisex studio: women, men and kids are all welcome.

Hair Color

How often do I need a root touch-up?

Most clients refresh roots every 4 to 8 weeks, depending on how fast their hair grows and how much contrast they have.

Balayage & Highlights

What's the difference between balayage and highlights?

Highlights use foils for a brighter, more uniform lift from the root. Balayage is painted freehand for a softer, natural grow-out. Many of the best results combine both.

What is Air Touch?

Air Touch uses a blow-dryer to separate the finest hairs before lightening, for an ultra-soft, seamless blend with no harsh lines.

Perms, Keratin & Treatments

Are perms back?

Yes. Today's perms give soft texture, volume and modern curls, not the tight curls of the past.

What's the difference between keratin and hair botox?

Keratin smooths and reduces frizz for months. Hair botox focuses on deep conditioning and repair, leaving hair soft and shiny.

Blowouts & Styling

How long does a blowout last?

With a silk pillowcase and a little dry shampoo, most blowouts last 3 to 5 days.
