Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Moses Agentic LLC ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you visit mosesagentic.com, talk to our website assistant or phone concierge, buy our services, or work with us as a client.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, company, phone number, email address, the industry you work in, and what you write in our forms, chat or emails, or say to our phone concierge.
  • Payment information: when you pay, your card details are entered directly with our payment processor, Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. We receive the payment status, the amount, the last four digits of the card and your billing contact details.
  • Calls and meetings: calls with our phone concierge, and video meetings with our team, may be recorded and transcribed so we can follow up accurately. We tell you when a call is recorded.
  • Client information: when you become a client, we process the business information you give our digital executives to do the work you hired us for.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting and service providers automatically receive your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you visit. We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how the site is used.

2. How we use information

  • To answer your questions and requests and to give you quotes and proposals.
  • To provide, run and improve our services, including the digital executives and websites we build and operate for you.
  • To process payments, send receipts and manage subscriptions.
  • To send you updates you asked for. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep our services secure, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with others for their own marketing.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as much as they need to do their job:

  • Payment processing: Stripe, to take payments and prevent fraud.
  • Business tools: our email, customer database, workflow automation, website hosting, and voice and transcription providers, which store and move information on our behalf.
  • AI providers: the AI services that power our digital executives, which process information to perform the tasks you ask for.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, under confidentiality duties.
  • Legal reasons: when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our clients, our company or others.
  • Business changes: if our business is merged or sold, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

4. How information is disclosed

Information is passed to service providers electronically over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), through their secure systems and integrations. Our providers are bound by contracts or terms that limit their use of the information to providing their services to us. We do not publish client names or details without permission.

5. How we protect information

  • All traffic to our website and services is encrypted in transit with HTTPS.
  • Card payments are handled by Stripe, which is certified to PCI DSS Level 1. Card numbers never touch our systems.
  • Access to client and lead information is limited to the people who need it, using accounts protected by strong passwords and two-step verification where available.
  • Meeting notes and client records are kept in access-controlled systems and are not shared outside our team.
  • We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires, then delete it or make it anonymous.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you marketing. Email welcome@mosesagentic.com. Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have additional rights, and we will honor them.

7. Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies and similar storage to remember your language and preferences and to keep the site working. You can block cookies in your browser. Parts of the site may then not work properly.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced on our website or by email.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: welcome@mosesagentic.com. Moses Agentic LLC, New York.
