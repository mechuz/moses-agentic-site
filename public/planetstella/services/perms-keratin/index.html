Questions

Are perms back?

Yes. Today's perms give soft texture, volume and modern curls, not the tight curls of the past.

What's the difference between keratin and hair botox?

Keratin smooths and reduces frizz for months. Hair botox focuses on deep conditioning and repair, leaving hair soft and shiny.
