How monitoring works

Four steps, and none of them is a phone call.

  1. 1

    Delivered and connected

    The generator arrives with a fuel sensor on the tank. The driver connects it and checks the first reading before leaving.

  2. 2

    Watched

    The tank level reaches our office all day, next to how fast your load burns it.

  3. 3

    Booked before it runs low

    When a tank would drop below 30 percent before the next delivery day, the top-up goes on the route.

  4. 4

    You get a text

    A text tells you the day the truck comes. After it leaves, you get the gallons and a photo of the gauge.
