Inside the building

Everything
under one roof.

Start on the lanes, grab food without leaving your seat, end up in front of the stage.

01 · LanesLANES

Glow lanes with a stage at the end

Every lane lit with programmable LED, synced to the room. Order food and drinks from the scoring screen. Bumpers for the kids on Sunday, blackout mode for everyone else.

 02 · The Bar

Full bar, 40 feet long

Cocktails, frozen drinks, kosher wine and bourbon, zero-proof menu. Open till close.

 03 · The Stage

Live stage

Live bands, concerts, comedy and watch parties on a real stage with pro sound and lighting.

 04 · Kitchen

Food till close

Smash burgers, wings, loaded fries, pastrami. Strictly glatt kosher, delivered to your lane.

 05 · Private rooms

Party rooms with their own lanes

Three private suites, each with reserved lanes, a sound zone and a host.

 06 · Arcade & lounge

Arcade, lounge, big screens

Racing sims, air hockey, a wall of screens for the game, and couches to recover on.
