How to Make Your Hair Color Last Longer: 9 Stylist-Approved Tips

By Stella · November 8, 2026

You leave the salon with rich, glossy color, and then a few weeks later it looks dull, brassy or faded. The good news: most fading comes down to a few everyday habits, and small changes make a big difference.

1. Wait before your first wash

Give your color 48 to 72 hours to settle before you shampoo. The hair cuticle needs time to fully close and lock in the color molecules.

2. Wash less, and with cooler water

Every wash removes a little color. Try stretching time between washes with dry shampoo, and rinse with lukewarm or cool water. Hot water opens the cuticle and lets color escape.

3. Switch to color-safe products

Sulfate-free shampoos and conditioners made for color-treated hair are gentler on your color. Ask Stella which products fit your hair type.

4. Use heat protection every time

Hot tools fade color and dry out hair. Apply a heat protectant before blow-drying, curling or straightening, and turn the temperature down when you can.

5. Protect it from the sun

UV rays fade color just like they fade fabric. Wear a hat for long days outside or use a hair product with UV protection.

6. Rinse before swimming

Wet your hair with clean water and apply a little conditioner before you swim. Hair that's already saturated absorbs less chlorine and salt.

7. Tone at home, gently

Blondes can use a purple shampoo once a week to cancel brassy warmth. Brunettes can use a blue-toned product. Don't overdo it, because too much can leave hair dull.

8. Book a gloss between color appointments

A gloss or toner is a quick service that refreshes your tone and adds serious shine. It's the easiest way to make color look freshly done for weeks longer.

9. Keep hair healthy

Healthy hair holds color better. A regular trim and a bond-repair or deep-conditioning treatment keep the cuticle smooth, so color stays put and reflects more light.
