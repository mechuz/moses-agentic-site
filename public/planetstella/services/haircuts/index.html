Questions

How often should I get a haircut?

Most clients keep their shape sharp with a cut every 6 to 10 weeks. Short styles, tapers and bangs need a trim more often.

Do you cut men's and kids' hair?

Yes. Planet Stella is a unisex studio: women, men and kids are all welcome.
