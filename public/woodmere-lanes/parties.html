Questions

Party FAQ.

How far ahead should we book?

Sundays and Motzei Shabbos go 4 to 6 weeks out. Bar and bat mitzvahs usually book 3 to 6 months ahead. Weeknights can often be done within the week.

Can we bring our own cake?

Yes, with a hechsher the Vaad accepts. Everything else comes from our kitchen. Ask the events team and they'll confirm the list.

What about separate seating or a mechitza?

The Private Suite can be split and we have a portable mechitza. Tell us when you request the date.

Is the deposit refundable?

Fully refundable up to 14 days before. Within 14 days it transfers to a new date within 6 months.

Do you do parties on Friday?

Daytime only, ending two hours before candle lighting. Great for school trips and camps.
