The architecture

Six parts, in plain English.

  1. A fast, simple website.Plain pages that load quickly on a phone at a job site. No heavy software to maintain.
  2. One event log as the single source of truth.Every fact is an event with a time and a source: a rental, a service hold, a pick-up, a drop-off, a fuel delivery, a payment promise. Nothing is overwritten.
  3. An availability engine.It reads the log and answers three questions: what is in the yard, what is free for these dates, and when the next unit comes back.
  4. Connectors.An email inbox reader for the billing inbox, a sync with the accounting system, and text or WhatsApp for bookings and confirmations.
  5. AI agents that read and draft, and people who approve.The agents read each ticket and invoice, match it to a unit and a rental, and draft the bill or the reminder. Billing approves before anything posts or sends.
  6. Security.Your data stays in your own accounts. The agents see only what the rules allow, and every action is logged.

Technical detail

Front end
Static pages and plain JavaScript. No framework.
Event log
Append-only events: rental, service, hold, pick-up, drop-off, fuel, promise. In this preview it lives in your browser and syncs across open tabs.
Engine
Small functions that turn events into answers. The same code runs the website, the desk and the office view.
Connectors
Inbox reader, accounting sync, text and WhatsApp messages.
Agents
Read, match and draft. A person approves every post and every send.
Access
Read-only by default. Each agent gets only the access its job needs.
