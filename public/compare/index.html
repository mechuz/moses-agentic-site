Which one is right for you.

Each option has its place. Here is when each one fits.

Choose Moses Agentic when

you want work done every day in marketing, operations or finance without hiring, training or managing a team, and you want it designed by people who have run businesses.

Hire an employee when

the job needs a person on site, a licensed professional, or judgment and relationships that only a person on your team can bring. Give them digital workers, and they get more done.

Use an agency when

you need a one-off creative campaign or specialized expertise for a short project.

Do it yourself when

you enjoy building tools and have the time. Chocham Academy teaches you how.
